# VisorUp - Marketing Brief & Campaign Kit

> **Plan. Ride. Explore.**
> The all-in-one route planner and riding companion built for UK motorcyclists.

*iOS (iPhone + CarPlay) and Android. Version 1.1.0. Bundle: `co.uk.visorup.app`.*

---

## 1. The One-Liner

**VisorUp turns "where should we ride this weekend?" into a tapped-out, twisty, fuel-planned route in under a minute, then rides it with you, keeps you safe, and lets you share the glory.**

Positioning: a modern, UK-first alternative to clunky legacy route planners and generic car sat-navs. Built by riders, for riders who care about the *good* roads, not the fastest line from A to B.

---

## 2. Who It's For

- **Weekend tourers** who chase the best roads, viewpoints and biker cafes.
- **Group riders** who need everyone on the same route with live positions.
- **Adventure & touring riders** planning multi-day trips with fuel, food and overnight stops.
- **New riders** wanting curated, proven routes rather than a blank map.
- **Safety-conscious riders and their families** who want crash detection and one-tap SOS.

---

## 3. What The App Does (Feature Inventory)

### Route Building
- **Tap-to-build routes** - drop stops on the map and VisorUp shapes the roads between them.
- **Road personality** - choose Fast, Twisty or Scenic routing.
- **Twistiness control** - dial curviness from Chill to Max; the router actively hunts out the bendiest roads and avoids motorways.
- **Auto fuel stops** - VisorUp inserts refuels based on your bike's tank and MPG.
- **Round-trip loop generator** - pick a distance and direction, get a scenic loop back to the start.
- **Typed waypoints** - tag each stop as Fuel, Food, Scenic, Rest or Overnight Stay, colour-coded on the map.
- **Trip planner** - reorder stops and get a live **total trip-time estimate** (riding time + time spent at stops).
- **Multi-day tour planning** - split a long tour into daily legs.
- **GPX import & export** - bring routes in, or export to share and back up.

### Discover & Ride the Best of Britain
- **61 curated iconic UK routes** across Scotland (18), Wales (14), Northern England (15), Southern England (9), the Midlands (5) and the Isle of Man.
- **1,800+ points of interest** - fuel, campsites, top roads, viewpoints, bridges, biker pubs, mountain passes, wildlife spots, waterfalls, castles and beaches.
- **Weather along the route** - see conditions at each point of your journey before you set off.
- **Fuel range alerts** - warnings tuned to your bike's tank size and economy.

### Live Ride Mode
- **Turn-by-turn GPS navigation** with speed, ETA and off-route alerts.
- **Offline turn-by-turn** - download routes and navigate with zero signal (cache-first).
- **CarPlay support** - iconic roads, navigation and roadside help on your bike's/vehicle's screen.

### Safety First
- **Crash detection & SOS** - automatic alerts to your emergency contacts.
- **One-tap location sharing** - "I'm OK" or "I need help" sends your exact position (with what3words) to your contacts.
- **Nearest hospital / A&E finder** and quick roadside help (999 and breakdown).
- **Roadside essentials hub** - the info you need when things go wrong, right on the home screen.

### Ride Together
- **Group Ride** - live location sharing on a shared map with simple join codes, so nobody gets left behind.

### Community & Motivation
- **Community feed** - share your rides and photos.
- **Challenges & badges** - complete themed bucket lists: *Scotland's Finest, Welsh Dragons, Northern Passes, The Twisty Ten, Coast to Coast, Peak Bagger.*
- **Leaderboards & kudos** - rank by curvy miles, total miles or rides, and cheer each other on.
- **Shareable ride cards** - auto-generated map + stats images, perfect for socials.
- **Planned-vs-actual overlay** - compare the route you planned against the ride you actually did.
- **Route library** - publish your best routes and clone others' into your own collection.

### Your Garage & Running Costs
- **Multi-bike garage** - store each bike with its MPG and tank size.
- **Maintenance tracking** - service logs, mileage/time-based reminders and a TCLOCS pre-ride safety check.
- **Expense & fuel logging** - track running costs and get an estimated real-world MPG.

## 7. Launch Campaign Framework

**Phase 1 - Tease (pre-launch)**
- "Britain's best biking app is coming." Drip the 61 routes as a map reveal.
- Collect emails / TestFlight sign-ups.

**Phase 2 - Launch**
- Hero message: *Plan. Ride. Explore.* App Store launch + Road of the Week series begins.
- Push the free hook: build a twisty route in under a minute.

**Phase 3 - Community flywheel**
- Kick off the **Twisty Ten Challenge** with a leaderboard and a physical/merch prize.
- Encourage shareable ride cards (built-in) to spread organic reach.
- Partner with UK biker YouTubers/clubs for Group Ride demos.

**Phase 4 - Retention**
- Seasonal challenges (summer coastal runs, autumn mountain passes).
- Maintenance reminders and fuel-cost insights as re-engagement hooks.
